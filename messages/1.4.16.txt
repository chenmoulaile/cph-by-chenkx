cph-by-chenkx 1.4.16
====================

Fixed:

* The edit card looks like v1.4.13 again. Diffing the whole file tree of
  v1.4.13 against v1.4.14 shows that only two files under Highlight/ changed,
  and the card stylesheet (test_styles.css) is byte-identical. The only
  difference in the edit card was whitespace in test_edit.html:

  - in v1.4.13 each <a> tag had whitespace around its label. minihtml
    collapses it to one space, and that space is what gives the chip its inner
    padding, so the coloured background is not flush against the text;
  - v1.4.14 packed the whole template onto a single line, which removed the
    padding and made every chip look cramped.

  Highlight/test_edit.html is restored to the v1.4.13 content and is now
  byte-identical to the file at tag v1.4.13 (blob sha
  dc7b0ad1cd034270b97719d56109e54cdb256786).

Notes:

* One correction to an earlier assumption: the newline after
  <div class="panel"> does NOT produce a blank line. The -run panel's card
  template (test_config.html) has always had that same newline and its cards
  are single-line, which shows minihtml collapses whitespace to a space rather
  than a line break. The blank line above the card came from the missing
  position-0 sentinel in the edit view (fixed in v1.4.15), not from the
  template.
* Regression checks: 155 -> 158.
